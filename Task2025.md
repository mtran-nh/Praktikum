# Wordle
Wordle is a popular word guessing game in which players try to guess a secret five-letter word within six attempts.

### Rules

- The goal is to guess the secret word, which consists of exactly five letters.
- After each guess, the player receives feedback for each letter:
    - **2 (=)**:    The letter is correct and in the correct position (green).
    - **1 (-)**:    The letter is in the word but in the wrong position (yellow).
    - **0 (.)**:    The letter is not in the word (gray).
- Each letter in the solution can only be counted once per guess.
- The game consists of three difficulty levels:
    - **Easy**: The player has no restrictions on the guessed word other than it has to be a five letter string.
    - **Normal**: The player must only use valid words from a predefined word list.
    - **Hard**: The guessed words must be valid according to a predefined word list. Additionally, all yellow and green letters must be used in the next guess.
- Only valid words from a predefined word list may be guessed.
- The game ends when the word is guessed or all attempts are used up.
- Example:
    - Secret word: `crane`
    - Guess: `reach`
    - Feedback: {1, 1, 2, 1, 0}
        - `c`, `r` and `e` are in the word but wrong position (1)
        - `a` is correct and in the correct position (2)
        - `h` is not in the word (0)
